## Notes for sending (not part of the email)

- **Clip: link it, never attach.** The tracker's own rule — a 20-second clip runs
  30–65 MB and Gmail stops at 25 MB, and a fat attachment from a young domain is a
  spam signal at every gateway it crosses. Upload, then paste the URL over `[LINK]`.
- **Attach only the PDF.** One page, no `.zip`, no deck.
- **The claim to keep straight.** "Built in" means their part is a row in the
  member catalogue with their rated values — it is not a claim that Nordic
  endorses the model. Say "built in", not "integrated with".
- **Send order.** The board lists Nordic 4th; the sheet is ready now, so send it
  now rather than holding for the queue.
